Scheduling data only

CareConnect is built around HIPAA principles from the start.

  • Patients see only their own visits

    Every request is tied to the signed-in account, never to an ID in the address bar.

  • No medical records stored

    We keep appointment times, providers, and visit types. Nothing clinical.

  • De-identified reporting

    Analysts work from data with patient names and contact details removed.

  • Sessions that really end

    Logging out revokes access immediately on the server, not just in your browser.
